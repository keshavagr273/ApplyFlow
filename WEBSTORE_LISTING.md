## SEO-Optimized Long Description (Detailed, structured, and keyword-rich)

ApplyFlow is your ultimate AI-powered career copilot. Supercharge your job search by automating tedious job application form filling and tracking all your submissions in one place. Whether you are applying on LinkedIn, Internshala, Unstop, or company job boards powered by Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, or SmartRecruiters, ApplyFlow handles the busywork so you can focus on preparing for the interview.

### Key Features:

🚀 **Smart AI Autofill**: 
Stop copying and pasting your resume details. ApplyFlow reads your parsed profile data and utilizes advanced AI context-matching to automatically fill out text inputs, dropdowns, radio options, and textareas on any ATS form instantly.

💼 **Multi-Platform Web Clipper**: 
Save job listings with one click. Our Clipper parses job postings directly from LinkedIn, Unstop, Internshala, Indeed, and more, automatically capturing the job title, company name, location, and description.

📊 **Automated Application Tracker**: 
Keep your job hunt organized. Track the status of every application (Saved, Applied, Assessment, Interview, Offer, Rejected) in a clean dashboard. ApplyFlow can even auto-confirm submissions from the page state.

🧠 **AI Resume Tailoring & Q&A Assistant**: 
Analyze job descriptions against your resume in real-time. Get instant match scores, identify missing skills, and generate tailored cover letters or custom essay answers directly from your sidebar.

📁 **Resume Version History**: 
Save up to 10 previously parsed resume structures. Swap profiles with a single click to autofill different positions using targeted experience and specialized skill sets.

🎨 **Harmonious Premium Light Interface**: 
Designed with clean, modern typography and a vibrant color system that makes navigating your job applications a delightful, visual experience.

### Supported Platforms:
ApplyFlow natively supports major job boards and applicant tracking systems (ATS):
- LinkedIn, Internshala, Unstop, Indeed, Naukri
- Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, BambooHR, and generic company sites.
